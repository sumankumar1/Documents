🔒 Privacy First

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026 · Effective: May 7, 2026

📌 TL;DR — The Simple Version

  • 🚫 We collect zero personal data.
  • 🌐 OgreSleep has no internet access — it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • 📱 Everything you enter (alarms, sleep logs, dreams, mood) stays only on your device.
  • 🔑 No account required. We don't know who you are and don't want to.
  • 💰 Subscription payments are handled entirely by Apple / Google — we never see your payment info.
  • 📦 You own your data. Export or delete it any time.

1. About OgreSleep

OgreSleep is a mobile application for alarm management, sleep tracking, mood logging, and dream journaling. It is designed to function completely offline. The application is available on iOS (Apple App Store) and Android (Google Play Store).

This Privacy Policy explains how OgreSleep handles (or rather, does not handle) your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information.

OgreSleep does not collect, transmit, store, or process any information on external servers. All data created within OgreSleep — including your name (if entered), alarm configurations, sleep logs, mood entries, dream journal entries, and app usage preferences — is stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device.

OgreSleep does not use:

  • Analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude)
  • Crash reporting services (e.g., Sentry, Crashlytics)
  • Advertising networks or tracking pixels
  • Third-party authentication providers
  • Any SDK that accesses the internet

The app's network permission (if declared in the manifest) is used only to display the in-app Help and Privacy Policy pages hosted on GitHub Pages. These pages are static HTML files and involve no tracking, cookies, or user identification.

3. Subscription Payments

OgreSleep offers optional paid subscriptions via in-app purchase. These purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). OgreSleep never receives, processes, or stores your payment information, billing address, or financial details. Please review Apple's and Google's respective privacy policies for information about how they handle payment data.

The only subscription-related data stored locally on your device is: the current plan identifier (e.g., "annual"), the expiry date, and a purchase token issued by the platform — stored securely using platform-level encrypted storage. This information never leaves your device.

4. Data You Create

OgreSleep stores the following data locally, solely to provide app functionality:

  • Alarm configurations (time, label, repeat settings, tone preferences)
  • Sleep logs (bed time, wake time, sleep quality rating)
  • Post-alarm mood entries (mood, energy level, dream flag)
  • Dream journal entries (text, mood tag, vividness score)
  • User profile settings (display name, avatar choice, sleep goals, theme preferences)
  • Achievement badges unlocked
  • App usage events (stored locally for your personal analytics only)

All of this data is under your complete control. You can export it as JSON or CSV, back it up to accessible device storage, or delete all data permanently via Settings → Delete All Data.

5. Device Permissions

OgreSleep may request the following device permissions. Each is explained below:

  • Notifications: Required to deliver alarm notifications and bedtime reminders.
  • Exact Alarm (Android 12+): Required to fire alarms at precise times.
  • Disable Battery Optimization (Android): Recommended to ensure alarms fire reliably in the background.
  • File Access / Storage (Android): Required only when you use the backup/restore feature to save or read a backup file.
  • Accelerometer / Motion (optional): Used only for the optional "shake to dismiss" alarm challenge. Never used for tracking.

No permission is used for any purpose other than stated above.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with anyone — because we do not have your data. All data is on your device and only leaves when you explicitly choose to export or share it (e.g., sharing a PDF sleep report via the system share sheet).

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Data is retained on your device until you delete it. You may delete all data by navigating to Settings → Privacy → Delete All Data. Uninstalling the app will remove all associated data (subject to platform backup settings — see below).

iOS Note: If you have enabled iCloud Backup, your device's app data (including OgreSleep's local database) may be included in iCloud backups, governed by Apple's iCloud privacy policy. OgreSleep itself has no control over iCloud. You can exclude OgreSleep from iCloud backups in iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Android Note: Android Auto Backup may back up your app's data to Google Drive. You can disable this in Android Settings → Google → Backup. OgreSleep itself does not initiate any cloud backup.

8. Children's Privacy

OgreSleep is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, there is no specific children's data at risk.

9. GDPR Compliance (EEA Users)

For users in the European Economic Area: OgreSleep does not act as a data controller or processor in the traditional GDPR sense, because no personal data is collected or transmitted to us. You retain full ownership and control of all data created in the app. Your rights under GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, portability) are exercisable directly through the app's settings.

10. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

OgreSleep does not sell, share, or disclose personal information to third parties for commercial purposes. California residents have no personal information held by us to request disclosure of, correction of, or deletion of.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes materially, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, if appropriate, include a notice in the app. Because we collect no data, most changes will be clarifications rather than substantive changes to data practices.
