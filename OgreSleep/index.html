Privacy Promise

Your sleep data is yours alone

We built OgreSleep from the ground up to never need an internet connection. No accounts, no servers, no analytics SDKs. Just you and your phone.

🚫

No Account Required

Open the app and start immediately. We never ask for your email.

🌐

No Internet Access

The app has zero network permissions. It literally cannot transmit your data.

🔐

Local Storage Only

All data lives in an encrypted SQLite database on your device.

📦

Take Your Data

Export everything as JSON, CSV, or a database backup. No lock-in.
