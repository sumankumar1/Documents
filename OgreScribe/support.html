Support

How can we help?

Answers to the most common questions about setup, reports, backups and Pro.

Getting started

How do I set up OgreScribe?
On first launch you pick your language, learn the three steps (record, review, share), download your speech pack, optionally enter your name and company, and allow the microphone. You can skip the speech pack and download it later in Settings.
What is the speech pack and why is it a download?
It is the on-device speech recognition model for your language: about 153 MB for English, about 360 MB for the multilingual pack and about 160 MB for a faster, less accurate multilingual pack. After the one-time download, recognition works fully offline. Use Wi-Fi if you can.
Which devices does it run on?
iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later) and Android phones and tablets. Recognition is faster on recent devices. A report of several minutes usually transcribes in well under its recording length.

Recording and reports

My transcript has mistakes.
Tap a line in the report to edit it, or tap its time stamp to hear what was said. Spoken text can contain errors, especially with names, numbers and noise: always review before sharing. Reports stay marked Draft until you mark them reviewed.
Why does a topic show as “Not mentioned yet”?
Your template (for example Property condition) expects some topics. If none of the expected words appear, OgreScribe flags the topic so you can add a note. It never fills in facts you did not say.
How do spoken commands work?
Begin a sentence with “Action” or “Hazard” (or the word for your language, such as “Maßnahme” or “Gefahr”), followed by a pause. For example: “Action: ask the manager to arrange a technician.”
Does recording continue when the screen locks?
OgreScribe keeps the screen awake while you record. On iPhone and iPad it can also continue briefly in the background. Phone calls and other apps can interrupt recording; OgreScribe pauses and lets you resume, and an interrupted recording can be recovered the next time you open the app.

Export, backup and moving to a laptop

How do I export a PDF?
Open a report, tap the share icon, and choose PDF report. You get a preview and can share or print it. Free PDFs carry a small “Created with OgreScribe” footer; Pro removes it and adds your logo.
How do I back up everything?
Settings → Backup and transfer → Create backup. You get one .ogrescribe file with every report, recording and photo. Save it to Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive, or send it by AirDrop or email.
How do I move my reports to another phone or a laptop?
Create a backup, send it to the other device, install OgreScribe there and choose Restore from backup. On a laptop, open the PDFs you exported; the backup file itself is for restoring into OgreScribe. Restoring never overwrites existing reports: conflicts are kept as a second copy, and restoring the same file twice changes nothing.

OgreScribe Pro

What is free and what is Pro?
Free: unlimited reports, recording, transcription, editing, PDF with footer, text export, backup and restore, up to 10 photos per report. Pro: your logo and business details on PDFs, no footer, custom templates, unlimited photos and a bulk export of all reports as PDFs plus an action-items CSV.
What happens if I stop paying?
Nothing you made is locked. You keep viewing, editing and exporting every report. Only Pro extras stop.
How do I restore purchases or cancel?
In the app: Settings → OgreScribe Pro → Restore purchases. To cancel, open your App Store or Google Play subscription settings (the app has a Manage subscription button). Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Troubleshooting

The microphone does not work.
Open your device Settings → OgreScribe and allow Microphone. You can also type notes instead of recording.
Transcription did not finish.
Your audio is safe. Open the report and tap Try again. If the device is low on storage or memory, close other apps first.
There is not enough storage.
Speech packs need up to about 360 MB and recordings about 2 MB per minute. Free some space, or remove a speech pack you do not use in Settings.
