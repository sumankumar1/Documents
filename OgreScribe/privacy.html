Privacy

Privacy Policy

Your recordings stay on your device. Here is exactly what the app stores and when it goes online.

At a glance

  • Recording, transcription and report building happen on your device.
  • OgreScribe has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own.
  • Your recordings, photos and reports are not sent to us or to anyone else unless you choose to share or back them up.

What is stored, and where

Reports, audio recordings, photos, your name and company (optional), a logo (optional) and your settings are stored in OgreScribe’s private storage on your device. They are protected by your device’s own security (passcode and device encryption). OgreScribe does not add separate encryption. The optional app lock uses Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or your device passcode to keep casual users out; it does not encrypt files.

When the app uses the internet

These are the only network connections OgreScribe makes:

  • Speech pack download. When you choose to download a speech pack, the app fetches model files from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Hugging Face can see the request and your IP address like any web server. No report data is sent. Each file is verified against a fixed size and checksum.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We receive no payment details. The app keeps a local note of your purchase so Pro stays unlocked offline.
  • Links you tap. Support, Privacy, Terms and Impressum open in your browser.
  • Sharing and backups you start. When you export a PDF, share text or create a backup, you choose the destination (for example email, AirDrop, iCloud Drive or Google Drive). That destination’s privacy policy applies.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record your walkthroughs.
  • Camera and photo library: to add photos you take or choose. OgreScribe does not scan your library.
  • Face ID / biometrics: only if you turn on the app lock.

Third-party components

Speech recognition uses open-source models and libraries: OpenAI Whisper weights (MIT), sherpa-onnx (Apache 2.0) and Silero VAD (MIT). Fonts are Noto Sans (SIL Open Font License). Licence texts are in the app under Settings → About and legal → Open-source licenses.

Your control

You can delete any report (moved to Recently deleted for 30 days, then removed), remove speech packs, or erase everything with Settings → Delete all data. Deleting the app removes its data from the device. Backups you saved elsewhere are not affected; delete them yourself where you stored them. Because we do not hold your data, we cannot access, correct or export it for you; the app lets you do that yourself. If you have questions, contact us at ogrebills@outlook.com.

Children

OgreScribe is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes
