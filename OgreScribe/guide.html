Guide

From walkthrough to report in seven steps

Everything you need to go from a spoken walkthrough to a PDF.

  1. Set up once

    Pick your language, download the speech pack (Wi-Fi recommended), add your name and company if you want them on reports, and allow the microphone.

  2. Start a report

    Tap Record. Give the report a title, choose a template (Maintenance visit, Property condition or General field note) and the language you will speak, then tap the red button.

  3. Walk and talk

    Describe what you see. Tap Marker to flag a moment, Photo to add a picture at that time, and Pause when you need a break. Say “Action: …” or “Hazard: …” to build your checklist as you go.

  4. Finish

    Tap the red square. OgreScribe transcribes on your device and builds the report. You can leave the app while it works.

  5. Review

    Read the report, fix any line (tap it), tap a time stamp to hear the original audio, fill in job details and add notes for anything flagged as “Not mentioned yet”. Then tap Mark as reviewed.

  6. Share

    Tap the share icon and choose PDF report, Branded PDF (Pro) or Plain text. Preview the PDF, then share or print it.

  7. Back up

    In Settings → Backup and transfer, create a backup file and keep a copy in Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive. To move to another device, restore it there.

Tips

  • Speak at a normal pace and keep the phone close. Wind and machinery lower accuracy.
  • Say numbers and units plainly (“two point four meters”). Recognition may write “2.4”; check numbers before you send.
  • Use Outdoor contrast in Settings → Appearance for bright sunlight.
  • On iPad and large screens, reports open beside the list.
OgreScribe helps you write down what you observed. You remain responsible for the report: it is not a certified inspection, legal record or compliance check.
