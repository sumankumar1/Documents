Built for the walk-around

From a spoken walkthrough to a report you can send.

OgreScribe is made for contractors, property assessors and anyone who documents what they see on site and would rather not rewrite their notes at night.

●

Record as you walk

Pause, resume, drop markers and take photos. Photos land next to what you were saying.

✎

Reports that stick to the source

Every line is what you said or typed, with the time you said it. Tap any line to hear the original audio. Missing topics are flagged, never invented.

✓

Spoken commands

Say “Action: …” or “Hazard: …” and the line joins your checklist or hazard list.

▤

PDF in one tap

A tidy PDF with photos, action items and your details. Pro adds your logo and removes the footer.

⇄

Backup and move to your laptop

One file with every report, recording and photo. Save it to Files, iCloud Drive or Google Drive, or AirDrop it.

☾

Light, night and outdoor

A paper look, a night look and a high-contrast outdoor look for bright sun. Works on phones and tablets.
