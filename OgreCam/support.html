The OgreCam field guide

Get back to the moment.

Quick answers for capture, party rolls, your gallery and sharing. If you write to us, include your device and iOS version—never send a private photo or payment details.

Start here

OgreCam is a camera and keepsake maker for a shared night out. Choose a camera look, take photos, and—if you like—start a party roll to give the evening a beginning and an end. Captures stay in OgreCam until you choose to save or share them.

First launch: allow Camera access when iOS asks. OgreCam needs it to show the viewfinder and take a photo. If permission was denied, open Settings → Apps → OgreCam → Camera, enable it, then return to the app.

Choose a camera look

Tap the look name or camera controls to choose a profile. The free looks remain available without Pro. Premium looks require an active, verified App Store entitlement. OgreCam displays the price and billing period returned by Apple before purchase.

Start and finish a party roll

  1. Open Party roll, choose a title and a roll size of 12, 24 or 36 photos.
  2. Add a venue or city if you want it printed on the keepsake. This is typed by you; OgreCam does not read GPS coordinates.
  3. Optionally enable gallery protection, then start the roll and pass the phone around.
  4. Finish the roll to choose favorites and make a memory receipt or a vertical Story collage.

When gallery protection is on, opening the gallery or finishing the roll asks iOS to authenticate with the device owner’s Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. This protects those actions inside OgreCam; it cannot stop someone from leaving the app. For a shared device, start Apple’s Guided Access before handing it over.

Select, save, share or delete photos

  1. Open Gallery and tap Select.
  2. Tap individual photos or use Select All. Use Clear All to reset the selection.
  3. Choose Save to Photos, Share, or Delete. OgreCam asks you to confirm a batch deletion.

Saving creates a copy in Apple Photos. Sharing opens the iOS share sheet, where you select a destination. Deleting from OgreCam does not remove copies you already saved or sent elsewhere. Each capture also retains the saved original JPEG alongside the processed OgreCam image; a single photo’s detail view offers the original when available.

Share sheet looks blank? Close it and try once more. If it is still blank, restart OgreCam and email us the iPhone/iPad model, iOS version, number of selected photos and whether the second attempt worked. Do not send the photos themselves.

Receipt or Story collage

After a roll, edit the receipt title and optional venue/city, choose up to six favorite photos, and switch between Receipt and Story collage previews. Use the matching share button to export that artwork, or share all photos in the roll. The collage is a still image sized for a vertical social Story; video export is not included in this version.

Purchases, restore and cancellation

Plans are offered through Apple In-App Purchase. The Pro screen shows the storefront’s live localized price and renewal period. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled through your Apple Account at least 24 hours before the current period ends. A lifetime purchase is a separate one-time product and does not cancel an active subscription.

  • Choose Restore Purchases on the Pro screen if a previous purchase is missing. Use the Apple Account that made it.
  • Choose Manage Subscriptions on the Pro screen to review or cancel an Apple subscription.
  • Apple handles billing and purchase verification. OgreCam cannot see your payment card details.

Troubleshooting

The viewfinder is black

Confirm Camera permission under iOS Settings, close other apps using the camera, then reopen OgreCam. Camera capture requires a physical device.

A capture does not appear in Gallery

Wait for the save indicator to finish, then reopen Gallery. OgreCam saves a photo before reporting capture success. If the library reports an error, keep the app installed and contact support before clearing its data.

Save to Photos does not work

Allow the requested add-to-Photos permission and check available device storage. You can still share from OgreCam without saving another copy to Photos.

My purchase is not recognized

Check your network connection and Apple Account, then try Restore Purchases. If the product is still unavailable, contact Apple for billing questions and contact us with the non-sensitive error text for app help.

Contact support

Email ogrebills@outlook.com. Include the device model, iOS version, app version and a short description of what you tapped and what happened. Never email passwords, verification codes, payment information or private photos.
