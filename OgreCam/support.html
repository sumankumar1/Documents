The quick answers

A few tiny fixes.

The viewfinder is black

Check Camera access in iOS Settings, close other apps using the camera, then reopen OgreCam. Capture needs a physical device. More camera help.

My photo hasn’t appeared yet

Wait for the save indicator to finish, then reopen OgreVault. If it reports an error, keep OgreCam installed and contact support before clearing app data.

The share sheet looks blank

Close it and try once more. If it stays blank, restart OgreCam and send us your device model, iOS version and number of selected photos. Please don’t send the photos themselves. See the full steps.

Where is my Pro purchase?

Use Restore Purchases on the Pro screen with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Restore or manage a purchase.
