OgreCam Support

Camera permission

Open iPhone Settings → OgreCam and allow Camera access. Return to the app and try again. A physical iPhone is required; the iOS Simulator has no camera feed.

Finding photos

Open the gallery from OgreCam. Photos and completed rolls are kept on this device. If you enabled gallery protection, unlock with your iPhone passcode, Face ID or Touch ID.

Sharing and deleting photos

In Gallery, tap Select, choose photos or Select All, then Share, Save to Photos, or Delete. Deletion from OgreCam does not remove copies already exported to other apps. If a share sheet appears empty, close it and try again; include your iOS version when reporting the issue.

Party privacy and receipts

You can type an optional venue or city for a roll or receipt. OgreCam does not read GPS location. The receipt and Story collage can be shared separately. For guests using your phone, enable Guided Access before handing it over; the in-app gallery lock does not prevent switching to other apps.

Purchases

Open OgreCam Pro and tap Restore Purchases if a purchase is missing. You can use Manage Subscriptions on the same screen to change or cancel a plan through Apple. Prices and availability are set by your App Store storefront.

Contact

Email ogrebills@outlook.com. Describe your device model, iOS version, the step that failed and the error message. Do not email private photos, payment-card numbers or passwords.
