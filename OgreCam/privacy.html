Clear by design

Your pictures stay yours.

This policy explains what OgreCam keeps on your device, what happens when you export something, and how this support website is hosted.

The app in a nutshell: OgreCam has no developer-operated photo server, user account, advertising or analytics SDK. Photos and party details are kept in the local app library. OgreCam does not request GPS location. Apple handles App Store purchases.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Who is responsible

OgreCam is developed and published by Suman Kumar, an independent developer based in Germany. For privacy questions, email ogrebills@outlook.com. Full operator details are in the Impressum.

Information kept on your device

OgreCam stores captured photos, saved original images, edits and app-library records in OgreVault on your device. Records can include a photo’s capture time and camera look; party title, settings and optional venue or city; names and notes in your guest book; scheduled party dates; and your choices for a receipt, collage, book, album or portfolio.

You can add captions, guest names, a host signature, cover text, or optional portfolio details such as a name, role, bio and contact text. These details stay in OgreCam while you work. They may be rendered into a keepsake image or book if you choose that option. Photos you select for import are copied into OgreVault; OgreCam does not scan your entire Apple Photos library.

This information supports the app’s features. OgreCam does not transmit it to a developer-operated server. The app has no sign-in account, and the developer does not receive your photos, typed place, guest list, profile details or gallery contents.

Permissions and device features

  • Camera: used to display the live viewfinder and take a photo.
  • Photo picker: when you choose photos to import, the iOS picker lets you select the items OgreCam can use. OgreCam copies only those selections into its local library.
  • Photos: OgreCam requests add-only access when you choose Save to Photos. It does not need permission to browse your existing photo library for this action.
  • Notifications: if you enable a reminder for a planned party, OgreCam can schedule a local iOS notification. The reminder and plan stay on your device; notification access is optional and can be changed in iOS Settings.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or passcode: when you enable gallery protection, iOS checks that the device owner authenticated. OgreCam receives the authentication result, not biometric data or your passcode.
  • Location: OgreCam does not request GPS access. Venue or city is optional text you enter yourself and stays in the local party record unless you include it in exported artwork.

When you save, print or share

Save to Photos creates a copy in your Apple photo library. If iCloud Photos is enabled, Apple may sync that copy according to your Apple settings. When you share or print a photo or keepsake, iOS sends the selected item to the destination you choose. That destination may store, process or upload it under its own terms and privacy policy.

Review a keepsake before sharing: it can contain any guest name, caption, place, signature or portfolio contact text you chose to add. OgreCam cannot control copies outside its own library. Deleting a photo in OgreCam removes its local app copy, but does not delete a separate copy already saved or shared. Device backups may also include app data depending on your iOS backup settings. If you delete OgreCam, local app files may be removed; export anything you want to keep first.

App Store purchases

In-app purchases, product information, transaction verification, subscription status and restoration are handled through Apple’s StoreKit and App Store services. Apple may process account and transaction information to complete these actions. OgreCam does not receive your payment-card number. See Apple’s Privacy Policy and your Apple Account settings for Apple’s handling of that information.

Retention and your choices

Photos, imported copies, edits, party rolls, plans and guest details remain in OgreCam’s local library until you delete them or remove the app. Use OgreVault and the Party Roll screens to select and delete app records; save any photos you want to keep outside OgreCam first. You can control device backups, notifications and cloud-photo syncing in iOS Settings. Copies exported to other apps must be managed with those apps.

Children

OgreCam is a general-purpose camera and creative app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children on a developer server. Parents and guardians should use iOS purchase and device controls appropriate for their family.

This support website

These pages are static and do not use analytics, advertising pixels, embedded social feeds or tracking cookies. They are hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as visitor IP addresses, to operate and protect the service. For details, read GitHub’s Pages data-collection information and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Following an external link or opening an email link connects your device to the service you selected. That service handles the resulting request under its own privacy terms.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when OgreCam’s features or legal requirements change. The latest version and its update date will be available here. For questions, email ogrebills@outlook.com.
