Clear by design

Your pictures stay yours.

This policy explains what OgreCam does with information on your device, what happens when you export something, and how this support website is hosted.

The app in a nutshell: OgreCam has no developer-operated photo server, user account, advertising or analytics SDK. Captures and party-roll details are stored locally. The app does not request GPS location. Apple processes App Store purchases.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Who is responsible

OgreCam is developed and published by Suman Kumar, an independent developer based in Germany. For privacy questions, email ogrebills@outlook.com. Full operator details are in the Impressum.

Information kept on your device

OgreCam uses the camera to capture photos and stores the processed image, a saved original JPEG, and app-library records on your device. Those records can include capture time, the camera look selected, party-roll title and settings, and an optional venue or city you type. Your photo selections are used to render the receipt or Story collage.

This local information supports the app’s features. OgreCam does not transmit it to a developer-operated server. The app has no sign-in account, and the developer does not receive your photos, typed venue, or gallery contents.

Permissions and device authentication

  • Camera: used to display the live viewfinder and take a photo.
  • Photos: OgreCam requests add-only access when you choose Save to Photos. It does not need permission to browse your existing photo library for this action.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or passcode: when you enable gallery protection, iOS checks that the device owner authenticated. OgreCam receives the authentication result, not biometric data or your passcode.
  • Location: OgreCam does not request GPS access. Venue or city is optional text you enter yourself and stays in the local roll unless you export the related artwork.

When you save or share

Save to Photos creates a copy in your Apple photo library. If iCloud Photos is enabled, Apple may sync that copy according to your Apple settings. When you share a photo, receipt, collage or roll, iOS sends the selected item to the destination you choose. That destination may store, process or upload it under its own terms and privacy policy.

OgreCam cannot control copies outside its own library. Deleting a photo in OgreCam removes its local app copy, but does not delete a separate copy already saved or shared. Device backups may also include app data depending on your iOS backup settings. If you delete OgreCam, local app files may be removed; export anything you want to keep first.

App Store purchases

In-app purchases, transaction verification, subscription status and restoration are handled through Apple’s StoreKit and App Store services. Apple may process account and transaction information to complete these actions. OgreCam does not receive your payment-card number. See Apple’s Privacy Policy and your Apple Account settings for Apple’s handling of that information.

Retention and your choices

Photos and party-roll data remain in OgreCam’s local library until you delete them or remove the app. You can select and delete photos in Gallery, save copies to Photos, and control device backups and cloud-photo syncing in iOS Settings. Copies exported to other apps must be managed with those apps.

Children

OgreCam is a general-purpose camera and creative app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children on a developer server. Parents and guardians should use iOS purchase and device controls appropriate for their family.

This support website

These pages are static and do not use analytics, advertising pixels, embedded social feeds or tracking cookies. They are hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as visitor IP addresses, to operate and protect the service. For details, read GitHub’s Pages data-collection information and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Following an external link or opening an email link connects your device to the service you selected. That service handles the resulting request under its own privacy terms.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when OgreCam’s features or legal requirements change. The latest version and its update date will be available here. For questions, email ogrebills@outlook.com.
