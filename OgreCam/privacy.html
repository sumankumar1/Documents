OgreCam Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Photos and party rolls

OgreCam processes photos on your iPhone or iPad and stores processed photos, their saved original JPEGs, roll names, optional place names, and gallery data in the app’s local storage. Place names are entered by you; OgreCam does not request your device location or attach GPS coordinates. The developer does not operate a photo upload or account service for this release. If you use the iOS share sheet, the photos, receipt, or collage you select go to the destination you choose, subject to that destination’s privacy practices. Anyone using your unlocked device may be able to see the camera view; an optional in-app gallery lock uses your device’s Face ID, Touch ID or passcode. To limit access to other apps while guests use your device, you can enable Apple's Guided Access.

Purchases

Apple handles in-app purchases, payment, subscriptions and restoration. OgreCam checks verified purchase entitlements to unlock Pro. We do not receive your payment-card number. Apple may process purchase and device information under its own privacy policy.

Device permissions

Camera access is requested to take photos. Add-only Photo Library access is requested if you choose to save photos there. Face ID, Touch ID or passcode authentication is requested only if you use the gallery protection feature. You can review or revoke permissions in device Settings.

Retention and deletion

Photos remain in OgreCam until you delete them or remove the app, subject to device settings and iOS backups. Deleting a photo inside OgreCam removes its saved processed and original copies from the app library. It does not delete copies you already shared, exported, or backed up elsewhere. Apple provides controls for managing subscriptions and purchases.

Analytics and advertising

This release does not include a developer analytics SDK, advertising SDK or account system. It does not track you across apps. Apple may provide aggregated App Store performance and sales information to the developer.
