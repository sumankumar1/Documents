Your folders. Your pace.

The OgreClean field guide.

Start with a folder you know, review the scan, and choose what to do only when you are ready.

A careful three-step routine

From folder to clear next step.

OgreClean is designed to make each step visible, so you can stay in control of the changes.

STEP 01

Choose a folder

Use the system picker to grant access to a folder you want to inspect. OgreClean scans only locations you select.

STEP 02

Review the results

Check folder paths, measured sizes, and cleanup guidance. You decide whether anything should move.

STEP 03

Use the Vault

Move selected items into the Ogre Vault. Restore them later from the Vault if you need them again.

Restoring an item

  1. Open the Vault from the main navigation.
  2. Select the item you want back.
  3. Choose Restore and check its original location.

The Vault is a recoverable holding area, not extra disk space. Items stay on the same device while they are in the Vault.

Managing an OgreClean Pro purchase

Open Upgrade to see the plans and prices returned by your store. Restore Purchases checks the store account used for the original purchase. Manage subscription opens your Apple or Microsoft account settings.

Annual subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled in the store account settings. Review the trial and renewal terms shown by the store before confirming a purchase.
