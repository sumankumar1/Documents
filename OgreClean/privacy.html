A quieter kind of utility

Privacy, by design.

OgreClean is built to work locally. This page explains what the app accesses and what happens when you buy Pro.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Information OgreClean collects

OgreClean does not collect or send personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting service.

Files and folders

You choose which folders OgreClean can inspect using the operating-system file picker. File names, paths, sizes, and scan results are processed locally. The app keeps its schedule, activity history, and Vault manifest on your computer. Items moved to the Vault remain on your device and can be restored from there.

Purchases

Pro purchases are processed by the Mac App Store or Microsoft Store. The store handles your payment and account information under its own privacy policy. OgreClean does not receive your payment-card details.

Network access

OgreClean has no developer-run account or analytics service. The Mac App Store build uses StoreKit to load and manage purchases. Opening Support, Privacy, Impressum, or Terms links connects to the selected website.

Children

OgreClean does not collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text and date will appear on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ogrebills@outlook.com. Please do not email passwords, payment details, or private files.
